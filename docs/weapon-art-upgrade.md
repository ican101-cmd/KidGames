# Illustrated weapon upgrade

Six original SVG equipment designs add ink outlines, wood grain, leather wraps,
metal facets, gold fittings and a faceted fantasy crystal blade. Shop previews
use the same art as battle equipment. No external assets or build step required.

Weapon visual metadata specifies width, height and grip. Responsive sizing
anchors each weapon to the illustrated character's hand; bows grip at center.
Animation poses differ by attack type: light diagonal cut, heavy backswing,
horizontal thrust, raised wand, drawn bowstring and three-cut crystal combo.
Projectiles launch from the equipped weapon. Reduced motion limits swings.

Damage values, prices, item IDs, impact delays and fruit_* save keys are unchanged.
Tests cover all six equipped weapons at five viewport sizes and the existing
save, shop, math, battle and Boss regression suite. Physical iOS/Safari testing
and deployed-site verification remain manual.
